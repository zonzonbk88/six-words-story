A complete story in exactly six words, for the make.fun
`u-deranalabs-six-words-one-story-f982e410` bounty:

> Sold her piano. She stayed silent.

Two beats — an action, then a reaction. A marriage, a sacrifice, and
everything left unsaid: why the piano was sold, what her silence means.
Every word earns its place, and the story keeps going in the reader's head.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Python 3.13, standard library only. Writes the six-word story to
`/output/entry.txt` (34 characters, valid UTF-8, exactly six words).
